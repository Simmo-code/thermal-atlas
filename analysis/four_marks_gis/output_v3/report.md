# Four Marks 30-mile paragliding terrain analysis

## Completion statement
The complete 30-mile circle was screened at 10 m using server-resampled cells from the same Environment Agency 1 m DTM. 56 locations were then downloaded from the original 1 m DTM and analysed on a 4 m averaged working grid to suppress one-metre banks and ditches.

## Whitewool benchmark
Representative sustained slope: **11.9°**. Robust maximum local slope: **25.3°**. Median local slope: **9.8°**. Median 240 m drop: **55.7 m (183 ft)**. Continuous ridge width: **1196 m**. Dominant downslope aspect: **ENE (60°)**.

Pass threshold: representative sustained slope at least 11.7°, relief at least 44.5 m, continuous ridge at least 838 m, and at least 35% of the broader face at the Whitewool representative angle.

## Limitations
The DTM is bare earth and cannot establish current crops, fences, livestock, trees, buildings, ownership, permission or safe flying conditions. OpenStreetMap screening is a current obstacle pointer, not a substitute for official protected-site records, current aviation charts/NOTAMs, landowner contact, a field inspection or a formal club site assessment.
